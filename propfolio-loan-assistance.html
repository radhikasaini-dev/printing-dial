Am I Eligible?

Loan Eligibility
At a Glance

Wondering if you qualify for a home loan? Here are the basic eligibility criteria — and don't worry, our team will assess your specific profile for free.

👤
Age Criteria

Minimum 21 years at loan application. Maximum 65–70 years at loan maturity (varies by bank).

💰
Minimum Income

Salaried: ₹15,000/month net salary. Self-employed: ₹2 Lakh+ annual net profit (ITR based).

📊
Credit Score

CIBIL score of 700+ recommended for best rates. We assist clients with lower scores too via NBFC partners.

🏢
Employment Stability

Salaried: Minimum 1 year in current job. Self-employed: Minimum 2 years of business continuity.

🌍
Nationality

Indian residents and NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) are both eligible. Special NRI loan schemes available.

Is the loan processing fee really zero for Propfolio clients?
+
Yes, absolutely. Propfolio has negotiated zero processing fee arrangements with our banking partners for all our property clients. This is one of the key benefits of buying your home through Propfolio — you save ₹10,000 to ₹25,000+ that other buyers pay to the bank directly.
How much home loan can I get on my salary?
+
Most banks offer a home loan of 60x your monthly net salary. For example, if your monthly net salary is ₹40,000, you may be eligible for up to ₹24 Lakh. However, this varies by bank, your existing liabilities, and CIBIL score. Our advisors will give you an exact figure based on your profile.
What is the minimum CIBIL score required for a home loan?
+
Most banks require a minimum CIBIL score of 700 for the best interest rates. However, through our NBFC partners, we can assist clients with scores as low as 650. If your score is lower, our team will advise you on ways to improve it quickly before applying.
How long does home loan approval take?
+
With complete documentation, most of our clients receive in-principle loan approval within 48–72 hours. Final loan sanction and disbursement typically takes 7–15 working days depending on the bank and property verification process.
Can I get a home loan if I am self-employed?
+
Yes. We assist self-employed individuals, business owners, and freelancers. Income is assessed based on your ITR (Income Tax Returns) for the last 2–3 years. We work with banks and NBFCs that have flexible criteria for self-employed applicants.
What is PMAY and am I eligible for it?
+
PMAY (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana) is a government scheme offering interest subsidy of up to ₹2.67 Lakh for first-time homebuyers in the affordable housing segment. Eligibility depends on your annual household income and whether you have previously owned a pucca house. Our team will check your PMAY eligibility at no charge.
Do you charge any fee for your loan assistance service?
+
No. Propfolio's loan assistance service is completely free for our property clients. We are compensated by our banking partners, which is why we are also able to negotiate zero processing fees for you.
