Quality Business & Visiting Card Printing

A visiting card — also known as a calling card — is a small yet powerful marketing tool for businesses and professionals. It contains your name, contact details, and brand identity, making a lasting first impression.

At Printing Dial, we print visiting cards on premium 300 GSM paper with sharp digital printing in standard 3.5×2.2 inch size. Choose from textured, matte, or glossy finishes.

Yes! Printing Dial specializes in instant and same-day digital printing. If you have an urgent requirement — whether it's visiting cards, flyers, brochures, or letterheads — simply call us at +91 99906 98878, share your print-ready file, and we will do our best to deliver within the same day. We recommend placing urgent orders before noon for the fastest turnaround.

For the best print quality, please send your file as a print-ready PDF in CMYK color mode at a minimum resolution of 300 DPI. Make sure your design includes a 3mm bleed on all sides and all fonts are embedded. We also accept files in CDR (CorelDRAW), AI (Adobe Illustrator), and high-resolution JPG/PNG formats. If you're unsure about your file, our team will review it before printing and guide you accordingly.

Yes, Printing Dial offers designing services along with printing. If you don't have a ready design, our in-house design team can create one for you — including business cards, brochures, letterheads, catalogues, posters, and more. Just share your brand details, logo (if available), and requirements, and we'll take care of the rest. Custom design charges may apply depending on the complexity of the work.
